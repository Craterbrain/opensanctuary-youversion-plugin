✝ YouVersion Bible

Add YouVersion translations (the NIV, NASB, AMP and many more) to the Scriptures sidebar. Search them there like any other translation (John 3:16): the results show only the reference, and the text is fetched when you add one to the schedule or drag it there. It is kept only as long as the publisher allows, and is never saved into a schedule file.

Your own YouVersion Platform key

OpenSanctuary releases already include a key, so you don't need one. To use your own YouVersion app and the licenses you accepted, register at platform.youversion.com and paste its App Key here. It is kept in your computer's secure storage and is only sent to YouVersion; it is used instead of the built-in one.
